01 / THE CHALLENGE

One person.
More than one record.

Separate systems rarely describe the same entity in exactly the same way. A name may be spelled differently, a date may be missing, or identifiers may not travel between sources.

RecordLink compares available fields across datasets to surface candidate pairs and the evidence behind each score, so teams can make informed review decisions.

02 / WHAT YOU CAN DO

A considered workflow
for messy data.

From raw CSV files to reviewed candidate pairs, each step stays visible and in your hands.

01↓

Dataset Management

Upload CSV files, inspect their contents, map columns, and process records with validation.

02∑

Similarity Scoring

Compare mapped fields with weighted similarity heuristics and configurable match thresholds.

03⇄

Candidate Generation

Use blocking to find candidate pairs, then inspect scores and field-level comparison evidence.

04◎

Human Review

Confirm or reject candidate matches without changing the algorithmic classification.

05✓

Evaluation

Compare predictions with independent reference labels on candidate pairs and view evaluation metrics.

06☰

Audit History

Review recorded workspace actions, including matching, review, label, and export events.

Evaluation describes labelled candidate pairs; it does not measure recall for pairs blocking did not generate.
